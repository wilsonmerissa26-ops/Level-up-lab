## Teaching implementation

Eleven original circuits lessons cover complete paths, conductors and insulators, V/I/R quantities and units, current calculation, equation rearrangement, series and parallel rules, circuit diagrams, current conventions, meters, motor energy, variables and graphs, scientific argument, and mixed transfer.

Each uses one saved teaching block at a time, worked examples, supported practice, fading support, an independent or mixed check, and an open explanation. Open explanations retain exact wording and a review rubric. They are excluded from automatic right/wrong scores and appear in Parent View.

The interactive explorer calculates ideal-resistor behavior. It distinguishes calculated examples from measured lab evidence, handles unequal parallel branches, and preserves the fixed-voltage assumption. It is an exploration surface and does not create learner evidence.

The existing Day 2, Day 7, and Day 21 review scheduler is reused, with different prompts across windows. Day 21 includes transfer. These checks do not declare formal mastery.
